FAQ

Questions owners ask us

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Billing, tables, menu, stock and reports all work offline. You need internet for the first setup (we verify your email), to activate your licence, and for cloud sync, Google Drive backup, emails and updates. When the internet comes back, sync and backups catch up on their own.

What do I need to run it?

A Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit) PC or laptop. For bills, any printer that works with Windows — including 58 mm and 80 mm thermal printers.

Is the free trial really free?

Yes. Each computer gets 15 days with every feature and no payment details. When the trial ends, the app asks for a licence code — your data stays safe on your computer and everything is still there once you enter it.

How much does a licence cost?

Licences are for 1, 3 or 5 years per computer. Message us on WhatsApp or call +91 96731 59640 for today's price.

Is my data safe?

Your data is saved on your own computer. Google Drive backups are encrypted on your computer before they are uploaded to your own Google account, and cloud sync goes to your own Supabase project. Passwords, the PIN and the Admin Code are never stored in plain text. We never sell your data.

What if my computer breaks or I buy a new one?

Install the app on the new computer, choose Restore from Google Drive on the sign-in screen and sign in. Your hotel, menu, orders and settings come back. Then contact us with the new Computer ID and we will move your licence.

Can I use one account on two computers?

A hotel account is signed in on one computer at a time. Signing in on another computer asks first and then signs the old one out. Each computer needs its own licence.

Do updates cost extra?

No. New versions appear in the app's Notifications — one click downloads and installs them, and your data and settings are kept.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

Windows shows this for apps whose installer is not code-signed yet. Make sure you downloaded it from swary.in, then click More info and Run anyway.

Will you help me set it up?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp us and we will help you install the app, set up your hotel, and connect backups and cloud sync.
