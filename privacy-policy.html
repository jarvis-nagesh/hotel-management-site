Privacy Policy

Swary Hotel Management and swary.in · Last updated: 5 October 2026

Who we are

Swary Soft ("we", "us") makes Swary Hotel Management (the "App"), a Windows application that hotels and restaurants use for tables, orders, billing, menu, inventory, waiters and sales reports. This policy explains what data the App and this website handle, why, and the choices you have.

Contact for privacy questions and requests: Nagesh Patil, Swary Soft — iamnageshpatil@gmail.com, +91 96731 59640.

1. Data kept on your computer

The App stores your hotel's data in a database on the computer where it is installed: business details (hotel name, address, owner name, mobile number, email, tax number, UPI ID), tables, waiters, menu, inventory, orders, bills, sales reports and settings. This data stays on your computer unless you turn on one of the optional cloud features below.

Your login password, lock-screen PIN and Admin Code are stored only as one-way hashes, never in plain text. Sign-in tokens for Google and Supabase are encrypted with Windows' protection for the signed-in Windows user and are never sent to us.

2. Data that reaches Swary Soft

To run the free trial, licences and emails, the App sends a limited amount of data to services that we operate:

  • Licences and free trial: hotel name, owner name, email and mobile number; an identifier derived from your Windows installation (a one-way hash, not the identifier itself); the computer's name; and the App version. We use these to start the 15-day trial once per computer, activate and renew licences, move a licence to a new computer when you ask, and send renewal reminders and payment receipts.
  • One computer at a time: an account key stored in your hotel's database, an installation identifier and the computer's name, so the account stays signed in on one computer and you are told if another computer tries to sign in.
  • Emails: when the App sends an email — verification codes, security alerts, the welcome email, the daily sales report, licence reminders and receipts — it sends the recipient address and the email's content (for the daily report: the day's totals, category and hourly figures and waiter-wise sales) to our email service, which delivers it through our email provider. These are used only to deliver the email.

We do not receive your menu, orders, bills or other hotel records, except the figures included in emails you choose to receive.

3. Optional features you control

Google Drive backup

If you connect a Google account, the App asks Google for permission to see your email address (to show which account is connected) and for the drive.appdata permission, which only lets the App use its own private, hidden folder in that Google Drive. The App uses this access only to upload, list, download and delete its encrypted backups of your hotel database, and to show the storage used.

Backups are encrypted on your computer before they are uploaded, with a key that is protected by your login password and Admin Code. Swary Soft cannot read them, and they are stored only in your Google account. You can disconnect Google Drive in the App's Settings at any time, or remove the App's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The App's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the backup and restore feature. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to build profiles, and not read by people except with your permission, for security reasons, or where the law requires it.

Supabase cloud sync

If you connect a Supabase project, the App copies your hotel's records (menu, tables, waiters, orders and related data) to that project and keeps it up to date. The project is yours: you choose it and sign in to it with your own Supabase account. Swary Soft does not have access to it.

Daily sales report

The daily report is emailed to the owner's verified email address. You can turn it off in Settings.

4. Updates and this website

The App checks GitHub for new versions and downloads updates from GitHub. This website (swary.in) is hosted on GitHub Pages and asks GitHub's servers for the latest version number to set up the download button. As with any website, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you visit or download. This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

5. How we use data

  • To provide the free trial and licences, and to prevent misuse of licence codes.
  • To send the emails described above.
  • To answer you when you contact us for support.
  • To keep the App and accounts secure and to meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

6. Who we share data with

We use service providers that process data for us only to run the services above:

  • Supabase — hosts our licence, email and one-computer-at-a-time services.
  • Brevo — delivers emails sent by the App.
  • GitHub — hosts this website and the App's downloads and updates.
  • Google — only if you connect Google Drive backup, in your own Google account.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it or to protect our rights and the security of our users.

7. How long we keep data

  • Data on your computer stays until you delete it or uninstall the App and remove its data.
  • Google Drive backups stay in your Google account until you delete them or remove the App's access.
  • Licence records and payment receipts are kept while you are our customer and for as long as tax and accounting laws require.
  • Email delivery records are kept by our email provider for a limited period under its own policy.

8. Your rights

You can see and correct your business details in the App at any time. You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or raise a grievance, by contacting us using the details above. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Some records, such as payment receipts, may need to be kept to meet legal obligations.

9. Security

We use hashing for secrets, encryption for backups and stored sign-ins, and HTTPS for every connection the App makes to online services. No system is completely secure, so please keep your password, PIN and Admin Code private and keep your computer protected.

10. Children

The App is a business tool and is not meant for children under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our data practices change and change the "Last updated" date above.
