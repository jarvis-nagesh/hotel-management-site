FAQ

Questions about working with us

What does Swary Technology do?

We are a software development company. We build custom software, AI agents and chatbots, business automation, web applications, mobile apps and Windows desktop software, and we make our own products such as Swary Hotel Management.

Do you build AI agents and chatbots?

Yes. We build AI agents that answer customers, take bookings, follow up on leads and update your records, and chatbots for your website and WhatsApp that know your products and policies. They connect to the software you already use.

Can you build software just for my business?

Yes — that is what custom software development is. Tell us how your business works today and what slows you down, and we will suggest what to build, from a small tool to a complete system.

How much does custom software cost?

It depends on what the software needs to do. After we understand your requirements we share a clear quote and timeline before any work starts. Message us on WhatsApp or call +91 96731 59640 to talk about your project.

Do you work with businesses outside your city?

Yes. We work with businesses across India over phone, WhatsApp and video calls, and our software is installed and supported remotely.

Do you support the software after it is delivered?

Yes. We help you install it, train your team, fix problems and add new features as your business grows.
